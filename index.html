Candles for the moments that matter

Vela comes from the Latin word for sail, and maison is home. We make candles that carry you somewhere calm without leaving the room.

Each scent begins with one feeling and is built around it: fresh air after rain, a cup of coffee at dawn, something sweet and soft, a spice market at dusk. We pour in small batches so every jar is checked by hand before it leaves us.
